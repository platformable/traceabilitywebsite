# Open Banking Accelerator - Get Started Guide

Welcome to the **Open Banking Accelerator** platform! This guide will help you get started with exploring Open Banking regulations and tracking upcoming regulatory changes around the world.

## Overview of the Platform

The **Open Banking Accelerator** provides a detailed, interactive world map that highlights Open Banking initiatives and regulatory frameworks globally. With this platform, you can:
- View Open Banking regulations for 193 countries.
- Filter and compare **Market-driven** and **Regulatory-driven** Open Banking initiatives.
- Track upcoming regulations with a comprehensive timeline and table view.
- Download the global regulations map and tables for offline analysis.

## Key Features


### 1. **Regulations Map**
The regulations map gives you a visual overview of countries with active Open Banking regulations. Each country is color-coded based on its regulatory approach:
- **Market-driven** regulations (shown in orange)
- **Regulatory-driven** regulations (shown in blue)


To interact with the map:
- Click on any country to view its regulatory details.
- Filter by specific approaches (Market-driven or Regulatory-driven) using the filters on the top of each section if exist.
- Download the map for use in reports or presentations.

### 2. **Upcoming Regulations Timeline**

In addition to the existing regulations, the platform also provides a **timeline** for upcoming regulatory changes. This section includes:
- Countries that will have Open Banking regulations in the next **3 to 12 months**.
- Detailed information on what types of regulations are being planned or updated.

Also we provide a table in a separate tab as a raw data from the timeline but including more fields.


You can toggle between different time ranges (e.g., next 3 months, 6-12 months, after 12 months) to view country-specific information.

### 3. **Upcoming Regulations Table**
The **table view** offers a more detailed, structured list of countries and their regulatory statuses over time. 
- The table breaks down upcoming regulations by country and time periods (next 3 months, 3-6 months, 6-12 months, and after 12 months).
- Click on a country row to view further details about the type of regulation coming into effect.
- Filter the table by quarter and download the data for analysis.

## Getting Started

### Step 1: Explore Open Banking Regulations
- Navigate to the **Regulations Map** by selecting the _Regulations Map_ button.
- Apply filters to see which countries have Market-driven or Regulatory-driven regulations.
- Click on a country to view more specific information about the regulatory landscape.

### Step 2: Track Upcoming Regulations
- Switch to the **Upcoming Regulations** tab to view the timeline for new regulations.
- Use the filter to adjust the timeline to the next 3 months, 6-12 months, or beyond.
- Explore the detailed breakdown in the **Upcoming Regulations Table**.

### Step 3: Download Maps and Tables
- Use the download buttons on both the **Regulations Map** and **Upcoming Regulations Table** to export data as images or tables.
- This is useful for sharing insights, preparing presentations, or conducting offline analysis.

## Filters and Customization

### Regulations Filters
- The platform allows filtering based on **implementation approaches** (Market-driven or Regulatory-driven).
- After selecting your filters, click **Apply Filters** to update the map and data display.

### Timeline Filters
- In the **Upcoming Regulations** section, use the filters to adjust the timeline view based on the specific period you are interested in.

## Conclusion

The **Open Banking Accelerator** is a powerful tool for mapping and understanding global Open Banking regulations. Whether you are tracking current market trends or planning for upcoming regulatory changes, this platform provides all the tools you need for informed decision-making.
